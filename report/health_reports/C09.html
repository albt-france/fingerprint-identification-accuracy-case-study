Definitions. Measured like Fingerprint documents it: events identified with a deterministic signal (control set) are re-identified without it. False positive: recognised as another existing visitor (two people merged). False negative: a returning visitor treated as new (one person split). Rates exclude requests flagged as bad robots. The confidence score is 1 minus the estimated probability of a false positive. Intervals: Wilson 95%. "Real change" = two-period test with over-dispersion 1.3, p < 0.01. Private-browsing traffic cannot be measured this way, so the measured rates are optimistic when its share grows.
